Overview

Blow Out Birthday Cake Candles does not require an account, does not use cloud sync, and does not collect or transmit your personal information to our own servers. Everything you create — cake designs, messages, and photos — stays on your device unless you choose to share or save it yourself.

Microphone

The app uses the microphone to detect when you blow, so the candles can react in real time. Audio is processed entirely on-device and is never recorded, uploaded, or transmitted.

Camera

Camera access is optional and only used if you turn on the photo toggle, to capture a photo of you at the moment the candles go out. If this is left off, the camera is never accessed. Photos are stored on your device and are only shared or saved if you choose to do so.

Photo Library

If you choose to save a cake photo or card from the share sheet, the app adds it to your Photos library. The app does not read or access any other photos on your device.

Advertising

This app shows ads served by Google AdMob. Ads are requested as non-personalized, so they are not based on your advertising identifier or App Tracking Transparency permission. AdMob may still use limited technical information (such as general location from IP address) as described in Google's own privacy policy.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — advertising

Children's Privacy

Blow Out Birthday Cake Candles is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Data Deletion

Delete the app to remove all locally stored data. No data is held on external servers, so no further action is needed.
