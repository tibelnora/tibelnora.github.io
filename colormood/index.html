Color Mood: Journal & Relax

Mood journal · Color diary · Patterns · Relaxation

Color Mood is a mood journal and color diary built around one idea: your feelings have patterns, and once you see them, everything changes. Pick the color that matches how you feel, and Color Mood turns it into a personal mood reading. Day by day, your emotional story becomes readable.

What it does

🎨 Journal your mood in color

  • Pick a color from the color wheel or an emotion from a list, and get a mood reading through color psychology
  • Tag entries with food, body, feelings and relationships, and add notes, a photo or a hand-drawn sketch
  • Flip through your color diary as a 3D book

📊 See your patterns

  • See how your tags, weather and barometric pressure relate to your mood over time
  • Connect Apple Health to compare sleep, steps, exercise, heart rate and HRV with your mood
  • Import your history from Daylio, Pixels, Day One or any CSV app

🔥 Relax and let it out

  • Sit by a campfire that reacts to your breath, under a sky of stars
  • A lava lamp, guided breathwork tuned to your mood color, and candles you load with worries and blow out
  • Write it all on the Rant & Release pad, then burn it, or draw freely on the Doodle Pad

⌚ Log in seconds

  • Log your mood from Apple Watch: turn the crown to pick a color, add tags or a voice note
  • Quick-log from Home Screen widgets without opening the app

🎁 Little gifts every day

  • A music pick, a book and a recipe chosen for your mood that day

Free to download, with daily mood journaling included. Private and on-device, with no account needed. Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open the app → tap the menu icon → Settings → scroll down and tap "Restore Purchase". Your purchase will be restored automatically if you're signed in with the same Apple ID.

I forgot my journal PIN. How do I reset it?

On the PIN lock screen, tap "Forgot PIN?" and verify with your device passcode. You'll be able to set a new PIN immediately.

How do I delete all my data?

To delete an individual entry, open it and tap the trash icon. You can also swipe left on an entry in the Calendar day view. To remove all data, delete the app from your device.

Why isn't weather logging for my entries?

Weather logs automatically when you save a mood entry, but requires location permission. Go to iPhone Settings → Color Mood → Location → select "While Using the App".

Why isn't Apple Health data showing in Patterns?

Go to Settings inside the app and tap "Connect Apple Health", then approve the permissions in the Health app. Make sure you have at least a few days of health data recorded.

Is my journal backed up to iCloud?

No. All data stays only on your device — there is no cloud sync or account. Use the CSV export (Pro feature) to back up your entries manually.
